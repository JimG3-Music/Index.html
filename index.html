Melody

Tap piano keys to enter notes. Tap a melody item to select it. Double-tap a melody item to change duration. Default duration is a half beat. Use the duration controls to increase or decrease it. Press and hold a staff note for about 700ms to delete it.

Musical Staff

New rendering system: The melody is stored independently from the staff. The staff is displayed in visual groups of 20 notes. These are NOT forced musical bars. You can enter an entire song without the staff stopping.
